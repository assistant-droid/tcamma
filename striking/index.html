Striking

Muay Thai

Our adult stand-up class focuses on the art of Muay Thai. You'll learn proper technique for punches, kicks, knees, elbows and clinching, plus self-defense against common street attacks.

Every class includes warm-ups, technique and controlled sparring. It's a must for anyone who wants to get in shape, defend themselves, or fight in the ring.

Striking

Boxing

We don't run a stand-alone boxing class — our stand-up class is heavily influenced by boxing as well as Muay Thai. Expect to learn stance, footwork, angles, slipping and evading, and a wide range of punch combinations.

We train on heavy bags, focus mitts, speed bags, jump rope and the spider-web — preparing you for self-defense or high-level competition.

Striking

Bag & Pad Classes

Timed rounds on heavy bags, kick shields and focus mitts — solo and with a partner.

A great class for beginners to get comfortable throwing strikes, or for experienced students to sharpen timing, technique and flow. One of the best ways to get in shape or get ready for a fight.
