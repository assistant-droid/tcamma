About TCAMMA

The Academy

South Central PA's first true mixed martial arts academy — built for everyone from first-timers to fighters.

Our Academy

One Roof. Every Discipline.

Tactical Combat Academy brings MMA, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, striking, youth programs and strength & conditioning together under one roof in Greencastle.

Regardless of skill or fitness level, our programs are designed to help every student reach their full potential — whether that's getting in shape, building confidence, or stepping into competition.

Facilities

Built to Train

Over 1,800 SF of mats, 9 hanging bags and caged walls to simulate real competition — plus a weight room, lockers, sauna and seating for parents and guests. Cleaned daily.
