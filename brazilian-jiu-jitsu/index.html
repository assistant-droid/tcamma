Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Traditional BJJ in a gi (kimono). Students learn takedowns, sweeps, escapes and submissions.

One of the most important parts of traditional BJJ is learning to use the gi as a tool — collar chokes, sleeve grips and gi control that let you manage much larger opponents. The gi takes away explosiveness and athleticism, forcing you to rely on technique and leverage.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

No-Gi Grappling

Our No-Gi classes blend Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and wrestling. Students learn sweeps, escapes and submissions with proper positioning and leverage.

No-Gi sharpens speed and control without relying on grips — the perfect complement to gi training.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Gi or No-Gi?

Both. Tactical Combat Academy recommends that every new grappler train in both Gi and No-Gi jiu-jitsu — each one fills the gaps the other leaves.
