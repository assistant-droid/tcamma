Judo, Catch & Kali

Judo

Held once a week and focused on the pure art of Judo. Learn how to fall properly (breakfalls), foot sweeps and throws using the gi — with plenty of reps in a safe training environment.

A great way to round out your grappling or train as a stand-alone art. Our academy is recognized by USA Judo.

Judo, Catch & Kali

Catch Wrestling

Catch wrestling is a grappling art built on using whatever means are available — often called “the violent art” for its aggressive style. There are no illegal submissions, holds, pins or takedowns, which makes it extremely effective for self-defense.

Ours is an authentic catch wrestling program under Snake Pit U.S.A., the most recognized catch wrestling program in the world.

Judo, Catch & Kali

Combat Kali

Based on the Filipino art of Kali — a primarily stand-up art built around short sticks and knives that translates directly to empty hands. Students learn angles of attack, footwork, disarms and more, with elements of Wing Chun and Jeet Kune Do.

Scenario drills for extreme close-quarter attacks build sensitivity for real survival situations. A must for street survival.
