Our Programs

One membership, every discipline. Train in as many programs as you want — or focus on one.

MMA

Mixed Martial Arts

Our complete MMA program. Learn to move seamlessly between striking, clinching, takedowns and ground fighting — built on the Tactical Combat System developed by Josh Hummer.

Learn More
STK

Striking & Muay Thai

High-energy stand-up training rooted in Muay Thai and boxing. Punches, kicks, knees, elbows and clinch work — with drills that get deeper the more you train.

Learn More
BJJ

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Gi and No-Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu taught by IBJJF black belts. Learn leverage, position and submissions that let you control a larger opponent.

Learn More
YTH

Youth Programs

Confidence, discipline and leadership — taught in a safe, positive environment. Our kids learn to set goals, chase them, and achieve them.

Learn More
SPC

Specialty Programs
